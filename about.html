About Me

ENGINEERING PATHWAY

Mechanical Engineering student building a fabrication-first and embedded-focused skill set with structured software development training.

Current Track

My name is Jacob Hartel. I am pursuing a Mechanical Engineering degree from Missouri University of Science and Technology while completing coursework in Springfield, Missouri through Missouri State University.

I come from a hands-on background in fabrication, machining, and automation, and I focus on building practical systems that connect mechanical design with embedded control and software.

My long-term direction is engineering work where design, manufacturing, and control systems overlap. I am especially interested in projects that demand iteration, discipline, and measurable performance improvements.

Focus Areas

  • Mechanical design and prototyping
  • Fabrication and machining workflow
  • Embedded systems and controls
  • Applied software architecture
